# Narrator clips

Chatterbox clones a narrator from a short recording in this folder.

- 10–30 seconds of one person speaking clearly, no music, little echo.
- WAV or MP3. The file name becomes the narrator's name in Settings.
- Read in the tone you want: slow and low for horror, soft for sleep stories.
- Use only voices you have the rights to: your own, someone who agreed in
  writing, or a stock clip licensed for commercial use. Never a YouTuber,
  actor or audiobook narrator, even if the recording itself is free.

Settings > Voice > "Make voice samples" also puts five starter clips here
(starter_*.wav). They are made with Kokoro voices, which belong to nobody.
